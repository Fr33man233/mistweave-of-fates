# Mistweave of Fates（灰雾织命）试玩反馈模板 / Playtest Feedback Template

试玩链接：[GitHub Pages](https://fr33man233.github.io/mistweave-of-fates/)

请先确认页面显示的版本；在 V0.2 正式发布前，公开链接可能仍是 V0.1。请尽量按以下项目反馈，不需要每项都填写。

1. 你是否理解当前该做什么？最困惑的地方是什么？
2. 三个案件中，哪一个最想继续追查？为什么？
3. 谨慎/冒险选择的代价是否清楚、是否值得？
4. 你是否注意到时间、警方关注、线索和日志的变化？
5. 自由行动输入是否符合预期？哪些输入没有被理解？
6. 刷新页面后存档是否正确恢复？请描述浏览器和复现步骤。
7. 两条路径是否都值得追查？仪式风险、首次能力和永久死亡是否清楚且公平？
8. 请给当前原型的“继续游玩意愿”打分（1–5），并说明原因。

请不要在公开反馈中包含账号密码、个人地址、支付信息或其他敏感信息。

## English

Please confirm the version shown on the page first; the public link may still serve V0.1 until V0.2 is formally released. Answer any questions that are useful—you do not need to complete every item.

1. Did you understand what to do next? What was most confusing?
2. Which of the three cases did you most want to pursue, and why?
3. Were the costs of cautious and risky choices clear and worthwhile?
4. Did you notice changes to time, police attention, clues, and the event log?
5. Did free-action input behave as expected? Which inputs were not understood?
6. Did the save restore correctly after a refresh? Please include the browser and reproduction steps.
7. Did both pathways feel worth investigating? Were the ritual risk, first ability, and permanent death clear and fair?
8. Rate your willingness to continue playing from 1–5 and explain why.

Do not include passwords, personal addresses, payment details, or other sensitive information in public feedback.
